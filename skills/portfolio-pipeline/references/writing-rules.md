# Writing Rules

The reader is a business owner or decision maker evaluating whether to hire the studio. They are smart but not necessarily technical. The copy must communicate competence through concreteness, not adjectives.

## Voice

- First person plural ("abbiamo progettato", "abbiamo scritto"): the case study sells the studio's authorship. Emphasize what was created from scratch versus configured.
- Default language: Italian, unless the user or config says otherwise.
- Value first, technology second: explain WHAT IT DOES and WHY IT MATTERS before HOW IT WORKS.

## Banned patterns

Never use, unless immediately followed by a concrete proof:

- soluzione innovativa / innovative solution
- cutting-edge, next-generation, revolutionary
- seamless experience
- sito moderno e accattivante, design elegante e intuitivo
- esperienza senza precedenti

Prefer the concrete formulation:

Bad: "Una soluzione innovativa per la gestione dell'inventario."
Good: "Il sistema mantiene sincronizzate le disponibilità tra più store usando l'EAN come identificatore condiviso."

## Avoid AI-generated tells

These patterns make the text read as machine-written. Avoid them:

- Em dashes (—) and en dashes (–): use commas, colons, or separate sentences. Ranges use the plain keyboard hyphen (righe 2-16) or words (dall'80% al 140%). Discount figures are written out ("badge sconto 39%", not "−39%").
- Arrow chains (A → B → C) in prose.
- Rigid parallel triads repeated across sections.
- "Non è X, ma Y" used more than once per document.
- Fragment-style compression; write complete sentences.

Normal hyphens in compound words (e-commerce, mobile-first, compare-at price) and Markdown list dashes are fine.

## What makes copy credible

- Numbers that come from evidence: line counts, product counts, price points, section counts.
- Naming the actual mechanism: "i badge sconto si calcolano dal confronto tra compare_at_price e price" beats "badge automatici intelligenti".
- Admitting scope: what was standard, what was custom. A section listing features NOT worth highlighting increases trust in the ones that are.

## Case study structure (CASE-STUDY.md)

Use exactly these sections, in this order:

```
Project Identity
One-line Description
Project Summary
The Product
Design & Visual Identity   (when the design was authored by the studio)
Key Capabilities
Portfolio Differentiators
Engineering Highlights
Architecture Summary
Representative Stack
Visual Story
Hero Screenshot
Portfolio Card
Suggested Tags
Verified Facts
Unverified Information
Questions Worth Asking
Final Assessment
```

Length guidance: One-line max 25 words. Card description max 35 words. Hero subtitle max 40 words. Overview 120-200 words. Capability blocks 30-70 words each. Max 4-6 capabilities, 3-6 differentiators, 3-5 engineering highlights, 8 tags.

## Design rationale

When the studio authored the visual identity, the case study must explain design decisions with their reasons: why this palette, what each color's role is, where accents are placed and why, what the photography direction is. Ground every claim in the design system files, the CSS tokens, or the live product. "Il verde guida l'azione, il crema fa respirare, l'oro segnala il valore" is the register to aim for: roles, not adjectives.
